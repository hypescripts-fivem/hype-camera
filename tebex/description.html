Give your police force eyes on the city with a CCTV system built for serious roleplay servers. Authorized officers watch security cameras in banks, stores and shops from station terminals, steer and zoom every camera in real time, and identify unmasked suspects through face recognition.

37 Ready Cameras Pacific Standard, every Fleeca branch, Blaine County Savings, 24/7, LTD Gasoline, Rob's Liquor and Vangelico come preconfigured, grouped into categories. Adding your own takes a single line, and a built-in position tool copies the coordinates for you.
Pan, Tilt & Zoom Steer every camera with WASD and zoom smoothly with the mouse wheel. Rotation slows down as you zoom in for precise control, and each camera can have its own field of view and turning limits.
Face Recognition Hold the crosshair on a player to scan their face and pull their record from the database: name, date of birth, gender, nationality, ID number, occupation and phone. Choose exactly which fields are shown.
Masks & Disguises Masked players, face-covering helmets and glasses, and players facing away from the camera cannot be identified. Recognition range grows as you zoom in, so distant suspects need a closer look.
Detection Log Every identified and masked subject is written to an on-screen detection log with time and camera, so officers keep track of who passed by while they switch between feeds.
Destructible Cameras Criminals can shoot the cameras out. A damaged camera sparks, goes offline for a configurable time and shows a live countdown until it comes back. Existing map CCTV props are used, and one is placed where the map has none.
Live Viewing Terminals Station computers open a searchable camera list with category filters and online status. Jump between cameras with a realistic connecting and signal lost flow, and walking away from the terminal closes the viewer.
Immersive Interface An authentic CCTV look with a security camera filter, timestamp, compass, pan, tilt and zoom readouts, a zoom scale and a scanning crosshair that reacts to the recognition state.
Access Control Decide which jobs and grades may watch the cameras, with an optional on-duty requirement and an admin ace bypass. Every permission check, recognition request and camera hit is validated on the server.
Developer Friendly Client and server exports to open the viewer, switch cameras on or off and take them down during a robbery, a camera destroyed event for dispatch and logs, and a live global state of offline cameras.
Wide Compatibility Detects ESX, QBCore and Qbox automatically. Supports ox_target and qb-target for the terminals, plus ox_lib, okokNotify and ps-ui notifications.
Highly Configurable Adjust cameras, categories, terminals, controls, zoom limits, recognition range and scan time, mask lists, the picture filter, date format, clock mode and much more. English and Turkish locales are included and new languages can be added easily.
Optimized Performance Built with performance in mind. Nothing runs while the cameras are closed except a light nearby check for shootable cameras, and the viewer only sends interface updates when something actually changes.
